# Cefla Finishing

**The most trusted finishing partner, in everything you see.**

Cefla Finishing is the Business Unit of the [Cefla](https://www.cefla.com) Group dedicated to surface finishing technologies. We design and manufacture solutions for painting, decoration and industrial digital printing: from a single machine to a complete turnkey finishing line.

---

## Who we are

With over 70 years of experience, Cefla Finishing is among the world leaders in the finishing of wood and non-wood surfaces. We work with customers of every size, all over the world, supported by a team of researchers, designers, engineers, process experts, software specialists and qualified technicians who build tailor-made solutions.

Our approach starts from a simple question: *what are our customers really looking for?* The answer is always the same: greater efficiency, more flexibility, better performance.

## What we do

Equipment and systems for the finishing of a wide range of materials:

- Wood and wood derivatives
- Glass
- Plastic
- Ceramics
- Fibre cement
- Composite materials
- Metal
- Cardboard and corrugated packaging

Serving industries such as furniture, building, automotive and aerospace, packaging and general industry.

## Innovation

- Patented technologies and continuous research
- Industrial digital printing that integrates into digital or hybrid lines
- Process digitalisation in line with Industry 4.0 principles
- One of the largest finishing laboratories in the world, at our headquarters in Imola

## The Cefla Group

Cefla was founded in Imola in 1932 as a cooperative. Today it is an international multi-business group operating in four areas:

| Business Unit | Field |
|---|---|
| **Finishing** | Painting, decoration and surface finishing systems |
| **Engineering** | Civil and industrial plants |
| **Medical Equipment** | Dental equipment |
| **Lighting** | LED lighting solutions |

## Global presence

Our headquarters are in Imola (Bologna), with production sites and subsidiaries in Europe, the United States and Asia. Internationalisation, digitalisation and servitisation are the principles through which the Group creates value over time.
